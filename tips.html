WORK SMARTER, NOT JUST LONGER

Study Tips That Actually Help 💡

Studying doesn't have to mean staring at notes for hours. Try these strategies to make your study time more useful.

⏱️

Try Focus Sessions

Work on one task for a focused period, then take a short break before starting again.

🧠

Practice Active Recall

Close your notes and try to explain a concept from memory. Check your notes afterward.

🗓️

Plan Ahead

Write down tests, projects, and assignments so you can spread your work across several days.

📝

Practice, Don't Just Read

Answer practice questions, solve example problems, or quiz yourself on key terms.

📵

Reduce Distractions

Put unnecessary notifications away and choose a study space where you can concentrate.

🌙

Take Care of Yourself

Take breaks, drink water, eat regularly, and get enough sleep to support learning.

A SIMPLE ROUTINE

Build a study routine

Try these steps whenever you sit down to study.

  1. Choose one clear goal for your study session.
  2. Gather the notes, books, and materials you need.
  3. Work on your goal without unnecessary distractions.
  4. Test yourself on what you learned.
  5. Write down what to review next time.
